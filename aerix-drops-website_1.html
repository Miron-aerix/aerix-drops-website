    What the utility uses – and what it doesn't claim

    We want players to understand where the information comes from before they rely on it.

    VOD Review: your footage stays local

    The VOD Review works with gameplay videos you select. It extracts timestamped screenshots on your device and lets you add observations and ratings.

    • No video upload is required.
    • No server or API key is required.
    • It does not automatically interpret gameplay like a vision AI model.
    • It does not read official Fortnite replay data.

    Maps and strategy: estimates, not official stats

    Drop locations use the map image and POI information built into the utility. POI ratings, route suggestions and zone guidance are strategic estimates based on map notes and general competitive principles.

    • POI ratings are not verified live-match statistics.
    • Loot paths and rotations are planning suggestions.
    • Zone guidance is not a guaranteed prediction of future circles.
    • You can create your own gameplans.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is AERIX DROPS free?

    Yes. AERIX DROPS is designed as a free competitive utility for Fortnite players.

    Does AERIX DROPS belong to Epic Games?

    No. It is a fan-made community project and is not affiliated with Epic Games.

    Does VOD Review automatically analyse my gameplay?

    No. The current offline workflow extracts video frames locally. You inspect the evidence, add notes and rate gameplay phases yourself.

    Are POI ratings official Fortnite statistics?

    No. They are strategic estimates based on the map information included with the utility, not verified live-match or tournament statistics.

    Can I suggest features or report issues?

    Yes. Join the Discord community and share feedback, suggestions or bug reports with the AERIX DROPS team.

    How can I become a partner?

    AERIX DROPS is open to connecting with creators, teams, organisations and brands in the Fortnite community. Reach out through the community channels to start a conversation.
